Check a file's syntax, types and borrows.

Usage: enrang check [file] [flags]

The file's `.test.en` part, as `main.test.en` is of `main.en`, is checked with it
when it exists. With no file, `check` takes the entry of `enrang.json` in this
directory, whose `target`, `cpu` and `float` are the defaults of the flags below.

Flags:
  --target=<target>        x86_64-linux, x86_64-windows, i386-linux, i386-none or x86_64-none
  --cpu=<level>            A CPU level or an alias, as `enrang targets` lists them
  --float=<strategy>       soft, sse2 or x87
  --color=<when>           auto, always or never
  --error-format=<format>  human or json
  --emit=<what>            mir or summary: print the checked MIR or the contracts
  -h, --help               Print this help
[stderr]
