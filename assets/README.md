# The Enrang logo

The mark is one line that reads as an *E* and a *t*, which is how the ampersand began: `&` is a ligature of the Latin *et*, "and". `&` is also Enrang's borrow sigil, the symbol at the centre of its memory model. In Italian, *e* on its own means "and".

The amber is the colour of old monochrome monitors. The dot grid on the social card is the contact grid on the underside of an LGA775 CPU, the socket of the Core 2 Quad in [the family PC](../README.md#the-story).

The mark and the wordmark are drawn as plain geometry, so no font is needed to show them. Only the two lines of small text on the social card come from a font, DejaVu Sans Mono, converted to outlines.

Colours: ink `#16120C`, amber `#FFB000`, cream `#F6EFE0`.
